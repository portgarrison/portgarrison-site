Scenario: a CT / MRI modality loses its network path to PACS and the remote radiologist — the kind of break that, in a stroke or trauma case, delays reads and forces ER diversion.
Triggers both lanes at once so you can compare.
Without PortGarrison
Normal operation — images flow from the modality to the radiologist. Network path down. Images queue at the modality and the radiologist can't read. In time-critical stroke or trauma cases, this can force ER diversion.
With PortGarrison OCL
Sense Decide Enforce Degrade Report
Normal operation — images flow through the OCL to the radiologist. Failure detected at the OCL. Enforcement continues from cached policy; traffic fails over to an alternate path. Reads continue, and the event is logged.

What just happened

When the path failed, the unprotected device had no fallback — its data simply stopped moving. The OCL appliance runs a continuous control loop at the device edge, so it detected the break, kept enforcing from locally cached policy, and rerouted the device's traffic onto an alternate path — all without touching or reconfiguring the device itself.
